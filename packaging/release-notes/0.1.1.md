## What's new

- **Get names from your AI assistant**, on a recording with no names to suggest yet, now opens People on its **Fill from your AI** steps: copy the request, paste it into your assistant, then paste its answer back. Before, it opened the list and left you to find the steps.

## Install

Download **TapeUp.dmg** below and drag TapeUp to Applications, or:

```
brew install kkyrian/tap/tapeup
```

Already on 0.1.0? TapeUp updates itself when you're not recording, or use **Check for Updates…** in the ··· menu.
