## What's new

- **The Get names from your AI button now opens the steps.** It shows on a recording when TapeUp has no names to suggest yet. It now opens the People page at **Fill from your AI**: copy the request, paste it into your agent, then paste its answer back. Before, you had to find these steps yourself.

## Install

Download **TapeUp.dmg** below and drag TapeUp to Applications, or:

```
brew install kkyrian/tap/tapeup
```

Already on 0.1.0? TapeUp updates itself when you're not recording, or use **Check for Updates…** in the ··· menu.
