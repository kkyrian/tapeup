## What's new

- **Remove only the audio.** Once a recording has a transcript, a new button next to the trash removes its audio and keeps everything else. You can undo it right away. You can also right-click a recording to remove its audio.
- **Recordings without audio are marked.** The card says "Transcript only", and lists show a small page icon before the length.
- **The trash button now says what it deletes:** the whole recording, audio and transcript.

## Install

Download **TapeUp.dmg** below and drag TapeUp to Applications, or:

```
brew install kkyrian/tap/tapeup
```

Already on TapeUp? It updates itself when you're not recording, or use **Check for Updates…** in the ··· menu.
