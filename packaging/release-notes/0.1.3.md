## What's new

- **Speakers are labeled on their own.** Once a recording's transcript is ready, TapeUp finds who spoke when, so there's nothing extra to click. Imports still follow their Label speakers switch.
- **You're among the names.** The name you set on the People page comes first when you say who was there and when you name a voice. It's offered, not assumed, so you can leave yourself out of a meeting you missed.
- **Open at login starts on** for new installs. Turn it off in Settings any time.
- **The floating timer stays on one line** past an hour.

## Install

Download **TapeUp.dmg** below and drag TapeUp to Applications, or:

```
brew install kkyrian/tap/tapeup
```

Already on TapeUp? It updates itself when you're not recording, or use **Check for Updates…** in the ··· menu.
