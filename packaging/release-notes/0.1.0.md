The first release of TapeUp, a meeting recorder for Mac, built for you and your AI agent.

## What it does

- **Records both sides of a call** in any app: your voice and everything your Mac plays. No bot joins the call. Press ⌥⌘R mid-call to start, and small controls float over your windows to pause or stop.
- **Transcribes on your Mac** while you talk, in 52 languages.
- **Knows who said what.** Label speakers finds each voice, with a line it said and a button to hear it.
- **Says who was there.** Tap how many were on the call and type a few letters of each name.
- **Names your recordings.** Pick Claude Code or Codex in Settings and it titles each recording and suggests who was there. It stays off until you pick one; without it, Apple Intelligence titles them where your Mac has it.
- **Brings in old recordings** from files or folders, transcribed with progress and the time left.
- **Finds any meeting** by its title, the people in it, or the words said.
- **Hands it to your agent.** Every recording is a folder of plain files with an `AGENTS.md`, and TapeUp has a command line agents can use.
- **Keeps going** when AirPods connect or disconnect mid-call, and writes audio as it records, so a crash keeps the meeting.

Recording and transcription stay on your Mac. Only the agent you pick sees a transcript.

## Install

Download **TapeUp.dmg** below and drag TapeUp to Applications, or:

```
brew install kkyrian/tap/tapeup
```

TapeUp isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS stops it the first time. Click **Done**, then **Open Anyway** next to TapeUp in System Settings › Privacy & Security. It downloads its speech model once (2.3 GB, or 680 MB on Macs with less than 16 GB of memory).

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later; tested on macOS 27.

## Updates

TapeUp checks once a day and installs updates when you're not recording. Turn that off under Settings › Automatic updates, or use **Check for Updates…** in the ··· menu.
