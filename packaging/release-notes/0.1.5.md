## What's new

- **TapeUp remembers voices.** Name a voice once, and TapeUp fills in that name the next time it hears the same person. A name TapeUp fills in is marked, so you can play the voice to check it or pick someone else. When you correct one, TapeUp shows earlier meetings where it made the same mistake, so you can check those too. Voices stay on your Mac.
- **Free up space.** Choose **Free Up Space…** from the ··· menu to remove the audio from old recordings and keep their transcripts. It shows which recordings will lose their audio before you confirm. Lock any recording whose audio you want to keep. Recordings that still need a transcript or speaker labels keep their audio until those are done.
- **Rename someone everywhere.** Rename a person on the People page, and the name changes in every recording. You can undo it.
- **Naming a voice adds that person to the meeting**, and to your people list if they're new.
- **New commands for your agent.** The command line can now import and transcribe a file, list and name voices, edit your people list, and remove or lock audio. Agents can lock audio but can't unlock it. To see the commands, run `/Applications/TapeUp.app/Contents/MacOS/TapeUp help`.

## Install

Download **TapeUp.dmg** below and drag TapeUp to Applications, or:

```
brew install kkyrian/tap/tapeup
```

Already on TapeUp? It updates itself when you're not recording, or use **Check for Updates…** in the ··· menu.
