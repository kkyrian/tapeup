## What's new

- **TapeUp remembers voices.** Name a voice once, and later meetings fill that name in by themselves. A filled-in name says so: listen, and leave it if it's right, or pick someone else. When you fix one, TapeUp shows earlier meetings where it made the same guess, for you to check. The voices it remembers stay on your Mac.
- **Free up space.** **Free Up Space…** in the ··· menu lists the recordings that still hold their audio, filtered by age. The ones that lose their audio are listed first, with the same count and size as the button. Lock any recording to keep its audio. Recordings without a transcript or labeled speakers wait until they have them.
- **Rename someone everywhere.** Renaming a person on the People page carries through every recording and their remembered voice, with Undo.
- **Naming a voice adds them to who was there**, and a new name joins your people list.
- **More for your agent.** The command line can now import a file (it transcribes and labels speakers too), list and name voices, keep the people list, remove audio, and lock audio. Your agent can lock audio but never unlock it. Run `/Applications/TapeUp.app/Contents/MacOS/TapeUp help`.

## Install

Download **TapeUp.dmg** below and drag TapeUp to Applications, or:

```
brew install kkyrian/tap/tapeup
```

Already on TapeUp? It updates itself when you're not recording, or use **Check for Updates…** in the ··· menu.
